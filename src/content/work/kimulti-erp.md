## Context

PT Kunci Inti Transindo (Kitrans) ran sales, accounting and logistics on a desktop application. It only worked on office machines, so anyone on the road or at home was cut off from the data they needed.

## What I built

I designed and built Kimulti from the ground up in Laravel and Livewire:

- **CRM, shipment and accounting modules**, with asset management following on.
- **WhatsApp automation** that sends customers their invoices and shipment itineraries without anyone typing them out.
- **A tax module** that calculates tax and produces the reports automatically.

## Outcome

Sales, accounting and logistics teams could finally work remotely and from their phones, and a lot of repetitive messaging and tax paperwork went away.
