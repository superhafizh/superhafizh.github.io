## Context

Small and mid-sized shipping companies were running tracking, invoicing and fleet maintenance from Excel files passed around by email. Nobody had the full picture, and mistakes were easy to make.

## What I built

- A multi-module ERP in Laravel and AngularJS covering shipment tracking, invoicing and vehicle maintenance.
- Custom features for each client, gathered directly from them, from small truck fleets to large warehousing companies.
- An automated deployment process using SSH scripts, so updates reached production reliably.

## Outcome

Clients moved off spreadsheets onto a single system, and I ran the whole development lifecycle from requirements to release.
