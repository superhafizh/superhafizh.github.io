## Context

Pasarin was a marketplace built for people who already buy and sell on Facebook. Its customer-facing interface needed a rebuild, and the company wanted one visual identity across its products.

## What I built

- Worked in the front-end team that rebuilt the marketplace interface in Vue.js and Nuxt.
- Implemented a new company-wide UI framework together with the design and management teams.

## Outcome

The marketplace got a modern front end and a consistent look across the company's products. Pasarin later changed direction.
