## Context

PT Assist Indonesia Pratama sells a core banking suite to microfinance institutions. Each institution has its own procedures and calculation rules, and all of them answer to the OJK, Indonesia's Financial Services Authority.

## What I built

- Developed and maintained the suite, built on a custom PHP framework, across loans, deposits and accounting.
- Onboarded and trained six new institutions, adapting the platform to their procedures and calculations.
- Worked on the update of the financial reporting module when the OJK introduced a new regulatory scheme.

## Outcome

Six more institutions running on the platform, and clients stayed compliant through the regulatory change without interruption.
